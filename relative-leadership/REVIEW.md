## New findings

Joint-removal average exposure rises43.6987% ->47.9189%, so the measured loss cannot simply be described as more idle cash. Under the already-defined recorded-account log attribution convention, participation contributes+0.0718563, held-name mix-0.2257889 and execution-0.0282102, summing to the actual log wealth ratio-0.1821427. The execution term includes timing/marking effects and is not merely commission or slippage expense.

At the recorded-account symbol level, joint-removal contribution from sh601869 falls1,568,167.98 ->33,857.06 CNY and sh688498 falls594,894.51 ->-18,849.69. Gains elsewhere, notably sh688347, partially offset those losses. Net terminal NAV falls785,365.43 CNY. These are differences between two actual funded paths; adding back selected lost contributions would not be a feasible portfolio or a valid new result.

A matched656-cohort audit gives joint-removal pair-ranking accuracy47.3468% ->51.6702%, even while wealth falls. Conversely, common-five wealth improves while its broad matched-pair accuracy falls55.0305% ->52.3933%. Therefore short-window cross-sectional order accuracy does not, by itself, identify the payoff quality of the actual funded holding/exit policy. The diagnostic cohort population is not the same as executed opportunities and is not newly unseen out-of-sample evidence.

Union's largest positive account-contribution difference is sz300502 (+3,470,784.08 CNY); common-five's is sz300308 (+1,110,458.31 CNY). These improvements do not establish independence from optical leaders. The paired result is useful evidence but not a generalized alpha recovery.
